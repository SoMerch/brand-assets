## Long description

SoMerch is a software-powered corporate merch platform and service partner for distributed companies across Europe.

The platform helps teams plan, produce, store, and ship branded merch from one place. SoMerch combines curated product selection, in-house production, warehousing, stock visibility, approval workflows, and fulfillment operations for use cases such as onboarding packs, employee gifting, event kits, office restocking, partner gifts, and managed inventory programs.
