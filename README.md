# SoMerch Brand Assets

Official public brand assets and media resources for SoMerch.

SoMerch is a software-powered corporate merch platform and service partner for distributed companies across Europe.

We help teams plan, produce, store, and ship branded merch from one place.

---

## What this repository contains

This repository is intended for public brand and media resources, including:

- official SoMerch logo files
- product screenshots
- app store references
- short company descriptions
- basic brand usage rules
- partner and media reference copy
- social profile assets

---

## Brand positioning

**Tagline:** Merch that moves with your team.

**Short description:**

SoMerch is a software-powered corporate merch platform and service partner for distributed companies across Europe.

**Extended description:**

SoMerch helps companies manage corporate merch from idea to delivery. The platform combines curated product selection, in-house production, warehousing, inventory visibility, approvals, and EU-wide fulfillment in one managed workflow.

These assets may be used for:

- media references
