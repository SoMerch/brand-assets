# SoMerch Brand Guidelines

Basic public brand guidance for SoMerch.

This document is intended for partners, media, directories, launch pages, public listings, and internal references where SoMerch needs to be described consistently.

---

## Brand name

The correct brand name is **SoMerch**.

Do not use:

- Somerch
- SO Merch
- So Merch
- somerch
- SoMerch.co as the brand name

Use **SoMerch** as the product and company-facing brand name.

Use **somerch.co** only when referring specifically to the website domain.

## Brand positioning

SoMerch is positioned as a managed corporate merch platform and service partner.

The core idea is simple:

**Corporate merch, managed from idea to delivery.**

SoMerch is not only a product catalog, a print shop, or a promotional products supplier. It combines software, operations, production, warehousing, and fulfillment into one managed system for modern distributed teams.

---

## Core message pillars

### 1. Software-powered merch operations

SoMerch gives teams a clearer way to manage corporate merch workflows, including product selection, quotes, orders, stock, approvals, and fulfillment.

### 2. Built for distributed teams

SoMerch is designed for companies with remote employees, multiple offices, cross-border shipping needs, onboarding waves, event kits, and recurring employee gifting programs.

### 3. Curated product selection

SoMerch focuses on a trusted, curated catalog instead of overwhelming customers with thousands of generic products.

### 4. In-house production and quality control

SoMerch combines production know-how, quality control, warehousing, and operational execution to make merch programs more reliable.

### 5. EU-wide fulfillment

SoMerch helps companies ship branded merch to employees, offices, events, partners, and customers across Europe.

## Avoid this positioning

Avoid positioning SoMerch as only:

- a generic print shop
- a simple promotional products catalog
- a consumer gifting app
- a print-on-demand marketplace
- a cheap giveaway supplier
- a basic swag store
- a one-off merchandise vendor

SoMerch should be presented as a managed corporate merch platform and service partner.

---

## Tone of voice

SoMerch communication should be:

- clear
- practical
- confident
- operational
- modern
- trustworthy
- helpful
- direct

Avoid:

- exaggerated claims
- vague buzzwords
- unsupported promises
- overly playful wording in formal materials
- generic SaaS language with no operational meaning

---

## Suggested wording

Good examples:

- Corporate merch, managed from idea to delivery.
- One platform for merch production, storage, and fulfillment across Europe.
- Built for distributed teams, HR, operations, procurement, and marketing.
- Curated products, in-house production, inventory visibility, and EU-wide shipping.
- A better way to manage onboarding packs, employee gifts, event kits, and office merch.
- From product selection to delivery tracking, SoMerch helps teams manage merch in one place.

---

## Wording to avoid

Avoid examples like:

- The best merch platform in the world.
- Unlimited products for everyone.
- Instant global fulfillment for any order.
- Cheap promotional products marketplace.
- The ultimate swag solution.
- Revolutionary merch automation.

These statements are either too broad, too generic, or too difficult to support.

## Logo usage

Use only official SoMerch logo files from the brand assets repository.

When using the logo:

- keep the logo readable
- preserve the original proportions
- keep enough clear space around it
- use it on clean backgrounds
- do not recreate the logo manually
- do not modify the logo without approval
- do not use the logo in a misleading way

---

## Incorrect logo usage

Do not:

- stretch the logo
- rotate the logo
- crop the logo
- add shadows or effects
- change colors without approval
- place the logo on busy backgrounds
- combine it with other logos in a way that implies an unapproved partnership
- use the logo to suggest endorsement without permission
